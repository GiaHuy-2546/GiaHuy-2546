## 👋 About Me

I'm an AI student at the **Faculty of Information Technology, University of Science, VNU-HCM (HCMUS)**, interested in turning machine learning research and modern AI techniques into useful, reliable software.

My current interests include:

- **LLM Engineering:** inference optimization, fine-tuning, RAG, and agentic workflows.
- **Multimodal AI:** text-to-video retrieval, image-text embeddings, OCR, and speech processing.
- **Applied Machine Learning:** building end-to-end systems, evaluating models, and improving real-world usability.
